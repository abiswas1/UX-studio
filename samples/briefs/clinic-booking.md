Design an appointment booking flow for an independent physiotherapy clinic with 6 practitioners and 2 locations.

Most bookings happen by phone during opening hours, which ties up reception and loses after-hours demand. Patients want to see real availability, pick a practitioner they trust, and reschedule without calling.

The first release should:
- let new and returning patients book an appointment type with a practitioner at a location;
- show real-time availability and confirm instantly;
- handle rescheduling and cancellation within the clinic's 24-hour policy;
- send reminders and collect intake details before a first visit.

Constraints: responsive web (most traffic is mobile browsers) plus the clinic's existing iOS and Android app shells. Health data must be kept to the minimum needed. Many patients are in pain or have limited mobility when booking.
