name: Dose — medication reminders
platforms: [ios, android]
---

Design a mobile app that helps adults who manage several daily medications — for themselves or for a parent they care for — take the right dose at the right time.

Today people juggle phone alarms, paper charts and pill organisers. Missed or doubled doses are common, especially when schedules change after a doctor's visit, and carers have no easy way to know whether a parent took their evening dose.

The first release should:
- let someone add a medication with its dose and schedule in under a minute;
- remind them at the right time and let them mark a dose as taken, skipped or snoozed;
- show today's plan and a simple history;
- let a carer follow one other person's doses, with that person's consent.

Constraints: iOS and Android at launch. No medical advice or dosage recommendations. Must work offline for reminders. Accessible to older adults with low vision or limited dexterity.
